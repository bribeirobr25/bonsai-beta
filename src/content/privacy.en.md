
### 1. Controller
Bar-Bonsai (research project), Berlin, Germany. Email: [contact address]. No data-protection officer is appointed because the statutory thresholds are not met.

### 2. What Bar-Bonsai is
A non-commercial research project in its validation phase. There is no charge and no advertising. The site exists to test whether a tree record of this kind is useful to people who keep bonsai.

### 3. What we process and why

| Data | Purpose | Legal basis | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email address, sign-up time, language | Sign-in by link, providing your account | Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR | until you delete the account |
| Photos, dates, notes and tree details you enter | Showing your tree record to you | Art. 6(1)(b) | until you delete them |
| Consent state with version and timestamp | Proof of consent | Art. 6(1)(c) with Art. 7(1) | 3 years after account deletion |
| Server logs (IP address, time, address requested) | Technical operation, security | Art. 6(1)(f), legitimate interest in secure operation | max. 14 days |
| Usage events (pages opened, times of photo or note entries, returns), pseudonymised under an internal identifier | Research into whether the tree record is useful | Art. 6(1)(a), optional consent | pseudonymised until the project ends, at most 24 months; aggregate only thereafter |
| Share link (random identifier) | Showing a record to people you give the link to | Art. 6(1)(b) | until you revoke the link |

Without the optional research consent no usage events about you are stored; the site works unchanged.

### 4. Recipients and processors
Processors under Art. 28 GDPR: **Supabase** (database, sign-in, photo storage), Frankfurt region, EU; **Vercel** (hosting), execution region Frankfurt, company seat in the USA, transfers under standard contractual clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where applicable; **PostHog** (usage statistics), EU cloud, only with research consent, cookieless; **Resend** (delivery of the sign-in link), USA, receiving only your email address, under standard contractual clauses.

Date and category suggestions for a note are produced on our server by fixed rules; no external AI service is used for them.

Your data is not sold and not used for advertising.

### 5. Your rights
Access (Art. 15), rectification (16), erasure (17), restriction (18), portability (20) and objection (21). You may withdraw any consent at any time with future effect (Art. 7(3)) in Settings or by email. You may complain to a supervisory authority; in Berlin, the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

Export and deletion are self-service in Settings: the export contains all entries and photos in an open format; deletion removes account, entries and photos within 24 hours. Backups are overwritten within 30 days at the latest.

### 6. Cookies and browser storage
Only strictly necessary cookies (session, language). Usage statistics run without cookies and without cross-device identifiers.

### 7. No automated decisions
No automated decisions with legal effect are made. Date and category suggestions are stored only after you confirm them.

### 8. Shared records
A share link lets anyone holding it read your record. You can revoke it at any time. Please do not share photos of other people.

### 9. Changes
This notice carries a version number. On material changes we ask you to acknowledge it again at your next sign-in and, where affected, to consent again.
