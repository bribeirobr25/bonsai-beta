# W6 · Participant Access Plan v1.0

**The binding constraint on the entire project.** Every step after Step 0 needs
participants. W6 owns this problem.

---

## 1. What is actually needed

**8 people** with:

- ≈5+ trees
- a current recording system (Photos, spreadsheet, notes, paper, tracker)
- enough history on one tree for a real retrieval task
- willingness to be observed for ~2 weeks

**Not** a mailing list. Not 500 signups. **Eight qualified, reachable people.**

That number is worth holding onto: the constraint is **qualification and
reachability, not volume.**

## 2. The cohort mismatch

The workshop (~4 weeks out) is **zero-to-low experience**. Step 1 needs expanding
hobbyists. **The one source we have serves Step 2, not Step 1.**

Canon is also explicit that workshop activation is *artificially inflated* —
instructor present, guided, novel, social — and that validation opportunities are
**inputs, not dependencies.**

## 3. Routes, ordered by evidence quality

### R1 · Landing page → qualified funnel
Built to W3's message architecture; CTA is **"join a one-tree test"**, not a
waitlist. **Qualification questions are the point**, not a growth metric.

*Strength:* produces message evidence and participants simultaneously.
*Risk:* a self-selected cohort of the curious. **Record recruitment source; it is not Growth evidence.**

### R2 · Direct outreach to visible expanding hobbyists
People who publicly document collections already demonstrate the behaviour under
test. Approach individually, transparently, as research.

*Strength:* **highest qualification confidence** — the behaviour is already visible.
*Risk:* selects documenters, who may be the easy case. **Record it as a limitation, do not correct for it silently.**

### R3 · Clubs and study groups
Canon `OBSERVED`: recurring small-group learning exists. A club is a dense
concentration of exactly this cohort.

*Strength:* efficient; may reach non-documenters R2 misses.
*Risk:* one club is one context. **Actor existence ≠ willingness.**

### R4 · The workshop
**Step 2 only.** Cannot serve Step 1. Label any result `WORKSHOP-CONTEXT SIGNAL`.

## 4. Sequencing

Run **R1, R2 and R3 in parallel.** They are not alternatives — they select
differently, and using more than one is how the selection bias becomes visible
rather than hidden.

**Record for every participant:** recruitment route · tree count · current
system(s) · documentation posture (public/private) · geography.

Without this, a Step 1 result cannot be interpreted — a pass among public
documenters is a different claim from a pass among private spreadsheet users.

## 5. The finding hiding in this plan

> **If 8 qualified participants cannot be reached through any route, that is a
> distribution finding of the first order — and it arrives before anything is
> built.**

Canon already records that observable attention in this category is extremely
concentrated: **16 of 23 eligible iOS products have zero ratings**. Difficulty
reaching eight people would be consistent with that, and would bear directly on
`G11` and on W2's `VK6` (CAC/support consumes the niche).

**Do not treat recruitment difficulty as a logistics problem to be pushed
through. Measure it.** How many were approached, how many qualified, how many
agreed — those numbers are evidence about the market, not overhead.
