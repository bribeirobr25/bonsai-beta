# W6 · MVP Validation Strategy v1.0

**Opened:** 5 September 2026
**Authority:** subordinate to project canon. Does not select a product.
**Inputs:** W1 closed · W2/W3/W4 canon v1.0 · Cross-Front Reconciliation · `S1` authoritative · Step 0 run

> **W6 does not choose what to build. It chooses what to learn next, and the
> cheapest honest way to learn it.**

---

## 1. The single largest uncertainty

Canon §12: *"what is the largest uncertainty right now, and what is the smallest
instrument that resolves it?"*

All three fronts converged on the same answer:

> **Can a dedicated experience around one real tree create current differentiated
> value over Photos / Excel / notes, before collection migration or long
> historical accumulation?**

W2 `G1+G2` · W3 `Progress → Memory` · W4 `L2`.

**Everything else is downstream.** If this fails, `P1` consumer economics weaken
materially, Brand may not rescue it, and Growth must not scale discovery.

---

## 2. The resource constraint has been lifted — and that changes the instrument

The founder has confirmed that **software, a landing page and a web app are not
resource-constrained**. If building is the best route, it gets built.

Canon §12 says *"an MVP need not be software"* and prefers concierge/manual
instruments. **That preference was never about purity — it was about cost and
speed.** With cost removed, the selection criteria become:

1. **Time to credible evidence**
2. **Evidence quality** — does it produce behaviour, or opinion?
3. **Fairness of the comparison** — does it test the hypothesis, or the artifact?

### The decision

> **Build a minimal one-tree instrument. Keep it deliberately incomplete.**

**Why building wins here, despite the manual preference:** the test's
load-bearing measurement is **Stage B — the participant adding a second tree
themselves, unaided**. A concierge cannot measure that. Someone else doing the
work is precisely what Stage B removes.

**Why "deliberately incomplete" is not a compromise:** every feature beyond
capture, retrieve and see-change **contaminates the test**. If the instrument is
richer than the hypothesis, a pass no longer tells us the hypothesis was right —
it tells us something in the bundle worked.

> **The instrument must be able to fail.** A product designed to impress cannot
> falsify anything.

### Scope of the one-tree instrument

**In:** one tree · photos with dates · events/interventions with notes ·
chronological view · retrieval (find a past event/photo) · add a second tree.

**Out, deliberately:** collection management · reminders · care guidance ·
AI · comparable journeys · sharing/community · onboarding polish · accounts
beyond the minimum · anything requiring migration.

**Reminders are out even though canon shows they are valued.** Including them
would make a pass ambiguous between *"the record is worth it"* and *"the
reminders are worth it"*, and W3 rejects reminders as a master territory anyway.

## 3. Participant access is the first problem, and there is a cohort mismatch

Every step after Step 0 is participant-blocked. **This is the binding constraint
on the entire project**, and W6 owns it.

### The mismatch nobody has flagged

| | Requires | Available |
|---|---|---|
| **Step 1 · `L2`** | **≈5+ trees**, currently using Photos/Excel/notes/tracker, enough history on one tree for a real retrieval task | — |
| **Step 2 · `L1`** | first/second tree | **the beginner workshop, ~4 weeks out** |

**The only participant source anyone has identified serves the wrong step.** The
workshop cohort is *zero-to-low experience*; Step 1 needs expanding hobbyists.

**Consequence:** the workshop is a real opportunity for **Step 2**, and it does
not unblock Step 1. Recruiting expanding hobbyists is a separate, unsolved
problem — see `W6-PARTICIPANT-ACCESS-PLAN-v1.0.md`.

---

## 4. Two artifacts, two jobs, not to be confused

| Artifact | Job | Cannot do |
|---|---|---|
| **Landing page** | recruit qualified Step 1 participants; test entry message | validate `G1`/`G2`, retention, WTP, PMF |
| **One-tree instrument** | run Step 1 | prove product-market fit, or that this is the product |

W4: *"a strong landing result may justify recruitment/testing, not product
build."* W2: *"it should feed participants into Step 1 rather than substitute for
Step 1."*

**The failure mode to guard against:** a landing page that performs well is the
most tempting moment in this project to skip to `W7`. It measures **message
relevance**, which is the layer the evidence is already weakest about
distinguishing.

---

## 5. Sequencing

**Parallel now — nothing waits:**

- **A · Landing page** — recruitment instrument, built to W3's message architecture and W4's evidence ladder
- **B · One-tree instrument** — built to §2 scope, ready before participants arrive
- **C · Step 0 extension** — multi-source, multi-climate corpus; pure Track A, spec already written by W4

**Then, gated:**

- **Step 1** when ≥8 qualified participants exist and the F1 protocol is frozen with W2
- **Step 2** at the workshop, labelled `WORKSHOP-CONTEXT SIGNAL`, which cannot rescue a Step 1 failure

---

## 6. What W6 must not do

- select `P1`/`P2`/`P3`, an MVP feature set, pricing, a payment architecture, a channel or a geography
- treat the one-tree instrument as a product decision
- let a good landing result substitute for `G1`/`G2`
- change any threshold after outcomes are visible
- report `INVALID` executions as `FAIL`

---

## 7. Kill and escalation conditions

| Outcome | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Step 1 **fails** under favourable conditions | consumer `P1` economics weaken materially; Brand may not rescue; **reconsider the venture thesis, not the instrument** |
| Step 1 **passes** | proceed to F1 with W2, then Step 2; **still no product decision** |
| Landing page fails | a message failed — **not necessarily the mechanism**; do not escalate to more poetic language |
| Cannot recruit 8 qualified participants | **the binding constraint is real and unsolved.** That is itself a finding, and it changes the venture's feasibility more than any single test result |

That last row deserves emphasis: **if expanding hobbyists cannot be reached at
all, that is a distribution finding of the first order** — and it arrives before
any product exists, which is the cheapest possible moment to learn it.

---

## 8. Handoff conditions to `W7 · Product/UIUX`

`W7` may open only when canon §12's list can be filled in:

target user · the job · value proposition · hypothesis under test · why this
wedge · supporting evidence · **contradicting evidence** · economic logic ·
acquisition logic · brand principles · what the MVP must prove · **what it must
explicitly not attempt.**

> Canon: *"If those cannot be filled in, the preceding workstreams are not
> finished."*

Today, *contradicting evidence* and *value proposition* cannot be filled in
without Step 1.
