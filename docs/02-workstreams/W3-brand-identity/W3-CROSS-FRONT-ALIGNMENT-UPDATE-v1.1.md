# Bonsai · W3 · Cross-Front Alignment Update v1.1

**Date:** 3 September 2026  
**W3 status:** **READY FOR CROSS-FRONT RECONCILIATION**  
**Purpose:** record the final W2/W4 responses relevant to W3 after W3 v1.0 closure.

## 1. Closure decision

W2 and W4 reported **no material contradiction** requiring W3 to reopen broad Brand Territory exploration.

Therefore the W3 architecture remains:

> **KEEP TRACK → SEE PROGRESS → BUILD MEMORY → CREATE CONTINUITY**

with:

- `Journey → Learning / Comparable Journey` as the leading future differentiation hypothesis;
- Psychological Safety as a Brand Principle;
- Durable Trust / User-Controlled History as a Trust Principle;
- Stewardship / Living History gated on H5;
- Belonging through Trees parked on H6.

No final positioning, product, MVP, pricing model, channel or Growth loop has been selected.

---

## 2. W2 alignment incorporated into W3

### 2.1 Acquisition value, retention value and monetized value are distinct

W3 adopts the cross-front guardrail:

> **message that acquires ≠ value that retains ≠ value that monetizes**

`Progress` may be the strongest entry/acquisition territory while organization, retrieval or workflow relief may matter more to return and economic value.

Brand must not interpret success of an acquisition message as proof of the value that sustains use or payment.

### 2.2 Continuity has a real truth cost

Continuity remains W3's strongest long-term Brand Territory hypothesis, but it creates real obligations around:

- backup;
- recovery;
- export;
- migration;
- support;
- software maintenance;
- service durability;
- funding obligations after churn or product-model change.

Therefore W3 continues to prohibit literal promises such as:

- `forever`;
- `permanent`;
- `never lose your history`;
- `always available`;
- equivalent unconditional permanence claims.

### 2.3 Subscription × Continuity remains an open tension

Subscription may finance an ongoing continuity obligation, but may also be perceived as paying rent to access a history the grower created.

One-time/lifetime may reduce that resistance while creating finite revenue against long-duration obligations.

Payment architecture remains `UNVALIDATED` and outside W3 authority.

### 2.4 Durable Trust strengthened

W3 retains **Durable Trust / User-Controlled History** as a Trust Principle.

Export/data continuity should primarily be interpreted as a **trust/commitment concern**, not a proven acquisition trigger.

### 2.5 Comparable Journey sequence

W3 keeps Comparable Journey primarily in the territory of **Learning from documented experience**.

The cross-front sequence is:

1. corpus-density shadow test;
2. user utility / repeat behavior;
3. only then retention, contribution or willingness-to-pay effects.

Comparable Journey must not be promoted prematurely into AI advice, community consensus or recommendation.

### 2.6 P3 / practitioner economics

W3 supports `W2-001` for Cross-Front Reconciliation:

> **P3 · Teacher / practitioner layer — Candidate ceiling; potentially higher-value professional payer and a possible source of attestations. Economics remain `UNVALIDATED`.**

Practitioner / teacher remains an ecosystem, authority and possible payer actor rather than the assumed primary consumer.

## 3. W4 alignment incorporated into W3

### 3.1 W4-A

W3 and W4 jointly recommend **ACCEPT** for the distinction:

> entry/switching moments → commitment gates → activation/value moments → activation constraints

Export/data-continuity concern belongs primarily to the commitment/trust layer.

### 3.2 L2 is the primary shared Brand/Growth gate

The main cross-front question is:

> **Can a one-tree experience generate current and differentiated value over Photos/Excel/notes before requiring collection migration or historical accumulation?**

W4 explicitly accepted the W3 Brand kill condition:

> **If L2 does not generate concrete advantage over Photos/Excel/notes under favorable conditions, W3 must not attempt to rescue the thesis with Continuity, Journey or Story.**

This is now a formal W3 constraint.

### 3.3 L3 · Progression Artifact

W3 retains:

> **progress/story > prestige**

and:

> **contribution ≠ invitation for critique**

Progression propagation should not require rankings, prestige, follower dynamics, forced sharing or critique invitation to function.

### 3.4 L4 · Comparable Journey

W4 accepted the W3 framing:

> **Learning from documented experience**

Descriptive comparable cases and recommendations remain distinct authority levels.

### 3.5 L5 · Teacher / Club

Authority remains attached to the practitioner, teacher, club or institution providing it.

The platform must not appropriate that authority into generic claims of Bonsai expertise.

### 3.6 L6 · Transfer / Provenance

L6 remains strategic secondary.

Evidence and attestation describe confidence in claims; they must not become pedigree, prestige or tree-quality coding.

### 3.7 Psychological Safety

Psychological Safety remains compatible with W4 only when it means permission to learn, record uncertainty and participate without performing expertise.

It must not mutate into horticultural-safety claims.

### 3.8 Brand/message kill conditions

W4 accepted W3's Brand/message kill conditions A–G. W3 therefore treats them as aligned cross-front constraints going into reconciliation.
