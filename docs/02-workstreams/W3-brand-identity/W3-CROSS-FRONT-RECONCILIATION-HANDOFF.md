## 2. W3's reconciliation question

> **Can a venture whose immediate value is Progress credibly and economically grow into a trusted system of Memory and Continuity, and can preserved history eventually create enough differentiated Learning value to beat general-purpose tools and crowded bonsai trackers?**

## 3. Conflicts W3 wants preserved

1. **Continuity × Economics**: stronger long-term promise creates stronger long-term provider obligations.
2. **Memory × General Tools**: strong job does not prove dedicated-product advantage.
3. **Progress × Differentiation**: strong entry reward is easy to copy.
4. **Learning × Capture Burden**: richer context improves comparison but may make logging intolerable.
5. **Psychological Safety × Authority Demand**: beginners may want reassurance but ultimately prefer authoritative instruction.
6. **Durable Trust × Retention Economics**: export/control may strengthen trust while reducing captivity-based retention.
7. **Stewardship × Market Relevance**: cultural meaning does not prove ordinary consumer value.

## 4. What reconciliation must not do

- silently promote a W3 strategic synthesis into customer evidence;
- choose P1/P2/P3 because it fits Brand better;
- use Continuity as a promise without truth conditions from Economics/Product;
- treat Progress as differentiated just because it is relevant;
- treat Comparable Journey as validated;
- turn Psychological Safety into an expert-care promise;
- reopen visual identity before the product/validation logic is resolved.
