## Current high-level findings

- `OBSERVED`: growers maintain bonsai records in multiple forms, including dedicated apps and general-purpose tools.
- `OBSERVED`: durable app use exists in some cases, including multi-month, daily and multi-year paid use.

## Method warning

The W1 corpus combines:
- App Store / Google Play reviews,
- public forum feedback,
- targeted exit/persistence evidence,
- competitive positioning evidence.

It is **not a probability sample**. Counts in purposively enriched subsets must never be interpreted as market prevalence, churn rate, retention rate or willingness-to-pay rate.
