## Identity rules

1. Count products, not names.
2. Localized titles are one product when platform/developer identity matches.
3. Lite/full variants in the same product family count once for competitor identity, but may have separate rating listings.
4. Same name + different developer/platform identity can be distinct products.
5. Historical/defunct products remain in the registry because W1 studies the competitive history as well as current availability.
6. False positives are stored in `COMPETITOR-EXCLUSION-REGISTRY.csv`.
7. Narrative documents may cite the discovered lower bound but should not maintain a parallel master list.
