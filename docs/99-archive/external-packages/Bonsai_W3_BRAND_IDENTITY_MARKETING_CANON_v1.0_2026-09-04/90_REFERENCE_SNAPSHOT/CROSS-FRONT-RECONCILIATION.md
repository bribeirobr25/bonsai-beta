## The decision the fronts did not have

All three closed **before** the success ambition was set. `S1` (€75k annual
contribution) became authoritative on 4 Sep. That is new information, and it
changes three things — none of them a contradiction, because W2 explicitly
anticipated this case: *"fail S3 / pass S1 = plausible small business, not
venture-scale."*

### 1. `G11` now has a number

W2's `G11` **STRONG** rule requires the conservative eligible-payer case to
support **≥2×** the requirement. That was untestable while the ambition was
open. Under S1:

| Net contribution / payer | S1 needs | `G11` STRONG needs |
|---:|---:|---:|
| €20 | 3,750 | **7,500** |
| €22 | 3,410 | **6,820** |
| €25 | 3,000 | **6,000** |
| €40 | 1,875 | **3,750** |

**A market-ceiling estimate is now falsifiable.** It was not before.

### 2. `A2`'s rank is unchanged; its *meaning* is not

W2 ranks the professional payer second because it offers *"a higher-contribution
path than low-price consumer tracking"* and its failure *"removes the strongest
unproven higher-ARPA escape hatch."*

Under S1 that justification no longer holds — **consumer-only economics can reach
€75k without it.** But `A2` is simultaneously the **only identified route to the
S3 aspiration** the founder has stated.

**Reconciliation keeps `A2` at second priority and changes what its failure
means:**

| | If `A2` fails |
|---|---|
| **Before** | consumer must carry the venture; a viability problem |
| **Now** | **S1 is unaffected. S3 loses its only identified route.** |

That is a materially different finding to report, and it should be reported that
way rather than as an economic setback.

### 3. The €3/month shape is now known-viable-if-reached

≈€22 net × 5,000 payers ≈ **€110k** — clears S1. This does **not** select a price
or a model. It removes the question of whether the shape *could* work at all.

## Agenda item 6 · Cross-front contradictions — **NONE THAT KILL A LOOP**

Two tensions found. Both are productive and should be **preserved, not resolved**.

**Continuity vs. its own cost.** W3 leads long-term on Continuity; W2 shows it
carries a real service obligation; W4 treats export as a commitment gate. These
agree on substance and differ on emphasis. The binding rule is already written:
*Continuity may be a Brand territory only to the degree the economic model can
truthfully fund it.*

**Progress acquires, but may not be what pays.** W3's strongest entry territory
is Progress; W2's strongest economic hypothesis is organization/retrieval. Both
can be true. The guardrail holds: **message that acquires ≠ value that retains ≠
value that monetizes.**
