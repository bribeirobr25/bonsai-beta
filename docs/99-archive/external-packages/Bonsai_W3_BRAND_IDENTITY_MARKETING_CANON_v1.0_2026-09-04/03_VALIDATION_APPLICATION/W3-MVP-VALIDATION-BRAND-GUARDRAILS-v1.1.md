# Bonsai · W3 · Brand Guardrails for MVP Validation Strategy v1.1

**Date:** 4 September 2026  
**Purpose:** Brand constraints for choosing, presenting and interpreting validation instruments. This does **not** choose an MVP.

## 1. Principle

The instrument must test the value hypothesis without borrowing credibility from a Brand promise that has not been earned.

## 2. Message layers permitted in tests

### Closest to observed language

Prefer where possible:

- keep track;
- progress;
- my trees;
- collection;
- photos;
- reminders when contextually relevant.

### Marginal language — use as hypotheses, not as strong evidence

- record;
- notes;
- organize.

`one place` is a single observed instance and should not be treated as a strong customer-language anchor.

### Strategic synthesis

- Memory;
- Continuity;
- Journey;
- Durable Trust;
- Stewardship;
- Living History;
- Learning from documented experience.

### Do not use as current promises

- expert care;
- correct recommendation;
- safe technique;
- verified tree;
- permanent / forever;
- never lose your history;
- AI you can trust with your tree;
- learn from thousands of comparable trees.

## 3. Gate-specific Brand constraints

### One-Tree Dedicated Value (`L2 + W2 G1/G2`)

Test concrete current advantage over the user's existing workaround.

Do not add Continuity, Journey or Story language to rescue a weak current-value experience.

### First-Tree Inception (`L1`)

Use Progress + Psychological Safety.

Reassurance = permission to learn, not horticultural correctness.

### Progression Artifact (`L3`)

Preserve:

> `progress/story > prestige`

> `contribution ≠ invitation for critique`

Do not force sharing or introduce rankings/follower status to make the mechanism work.

### Comparable Journey (`L4`)

Current state:

> **`L4-A = MIXED · DENSITY RISK HIGH`**

Tier 1 density was not testable in the reachable single-source corpus. Do not imply that useful multi-climate comparable cases exist at scale.

Any prototype must separate descriptive similarity from recommendation.

### Teacher / Club (`L5`)

Authority belongs to practitioner/teacher/club/institution.

### Transfer / Provenance (`L6`)

Attestation increases confidence in a claim, not status of the tree. No pedigree/status coding.

## 4. Interpretation guardrail

Always distinguish:

> **message that acquires ≠ value that activates ≠ value that retains/expands ≠ value that monetizes**

A strong click-through on Progress does not validate Continuity. A strong retention result on retrieval does not prove the acquisition message. A payment result does not prove deeper Brand meaning.

## 5. Brand stop rule

W3 must not rewrite failed validation into a more poetic proposition. If the behavioral/economic foundation fails, the Brand territory must be downgraded or reopened.
