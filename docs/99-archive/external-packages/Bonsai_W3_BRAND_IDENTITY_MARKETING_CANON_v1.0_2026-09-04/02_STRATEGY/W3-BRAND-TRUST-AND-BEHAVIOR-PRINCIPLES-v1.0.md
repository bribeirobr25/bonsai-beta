# Bonsai · W3 · Brand Trust & Behavior Principles v1.0

**Date:** 4 September 2026

## 1. Psychological Safety

The Brand should make beginner uncertainty legitimate.

> **Permission to learn, not a promise of horticultural safety.**

Do not shame starter material, beginner vocabulary or imperfect progress.

## 2. Durable Trust

Longitudinal value increases the cost of platform failure and captivity.

> **Trust should come partly from reducing captivity, not increasing it.**

If Continuity survives, future product/economic design must confront export, recovery, migration, service durability and user control.

## 3. Authority attribution

The platform may identify who made or confirmed a claim. It may not turn that into generic platform expertise.

Acceptable conceptual pattern:

`owner-recorded · evidence attached · counterparty confirmed · practitioner attested · institution attested`

Not acceptable:

`verified tree · Bonsai-approved tree · expert-certified quality`

## 4. Status resistance

Social meaning should not be primarily produced by price, prestige, awards, institutional association, followers or pedigree.

Working principle:

> **progress/story > prestige**

## 5. Contribution boundary

Working principle:

> **contribution ≠ invitation for critique**

A person may document failure, death or progress without automatically asking to be evaluated.

## 6. Relevance before popularity

If tree-led relationships are ever validated, discovery should prioritize contextual relevance over follower counts or generic feed ranking.

## 7. Uncertainty is not a Brand defect

When evidence is uncertain, the product/Brand should preserve that uncertainty rather than manufacture confidence.

## 8. No narrative rescue

If behavioral value fails, deeper Brand language must be downgraded. Brand exists to make real value legible, not to substitute narrative for value.
