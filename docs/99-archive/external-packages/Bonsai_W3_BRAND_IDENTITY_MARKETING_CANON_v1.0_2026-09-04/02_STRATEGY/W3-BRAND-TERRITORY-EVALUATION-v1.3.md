## 2. System A · Progress → Memory → Continuity

### Progress
Early reward; close to observed language. Insufficient differentiation alone.

### Memory
Primary bridge from utility to meaning. Strategic synthesis on observed record/retrieval behavior.

### Continuity
Leading long-term hypothesis. More distinctive than tracking, but can only be claimed to the degree the operating/economic model can truthfully support it.

### Durable Trust
Truth condition for Continuity: avoid captivity and unsupported permanence claims.

## 3. System B · Progress → Journey → Learning

### Progress
Observed anchor.

### Journey
Strategic structure around longitudinal context; not literal customer language.

### Learning
Potential differentiated meaning without pretending the platform is the expert.

### Comparable Journey current state

`L4-A / G10-A` ran 4 September 2026 and returned:

> **`MIXED · DENSITY RISK HIGH`**

What survived:

- real progression writing carries relevant domain-model fields;
- intervention and photo evidence were 12/12;
- species and stage 10/12;
- Tier 2 matching can produce multiple cases in the single-source sample.

What did not clear:

- only 12 cases, below the 20–30 design floor;
- all from one author;
- climate had effectively one value;
- Tier 1 comparability could not be tested;
- state/outcome/intent were only 7/12 each.

Therefore `Journey → Learning` remains eligible but is **more conditional than before**. W3 may not present comparable learning as an existing scalable capability.

## 5. Binding kill conditions

### One-Tree Dedicated Value

> If a dedicated one-tree experience does not create concrete advantage over Photos / Excel / notes under favorable conditions, Brand must not rescue the thesis with Continuity, Journey or Story.

### Comparable Journey

If realistic multi-source / multi-climate filtering cannot create useful density, downgrade `Journey → Learning`. Do not compensate by broadening claims about learning, evidence or community.

### Authority

If a mechanism only becomes attractive when Bonsai appropriates practitioner/expert authority, it is Brand-incompatible at the current evidence level.

### Continuity

If the viable economic/operating model cannot fund the obligations implied by Continuity, Continuity must be downgraded rather than overstated.
