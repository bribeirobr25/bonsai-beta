**Date:** 4 September 2026  
**Status:** CANONICAL W2 GATE FRAMEWORK  
**Authoritative ambition:** S1 · €75k annual contribution

# 1. Evidence hierarchy

Prefer:

1. actual payment / renewal / contract / deposit;
2. actual switching or continued use while substitute remains available;
3. real workflow behavior;
4. revealed choice with meaningful friction;
5. stated WTP;
6. general interest.

Interest alone does not pass an economic gate.

`STRONG / MIXED / FAIL` apply only after a test is completed.

Untested = `UNVALIDATED`.

---

# 2. W2 gate register

## G1 · Substitute superiority
Question:
> Does a dedicated workflow beat the current workaround for a real pain-qualified segment?

Proposed `STRONG`:
- ≥50% continue without researcher prompting;
- ≥30% make a meaningful real workflow shift.

`MIXED`:
- 25–49% continue; or
- 15–29% shift.

`FAIL`:
- <25% continue; or
- <15% shift,
despite pre-qualified pain.

Failure scope:
> consumer segment/candidate, not whole venture by itself.

---

## G2 · Time to differentiated current value
Question:
> Does meaningful value arrive before long-history payoff?

Proposed `STRONG`:
- ≥50% experience differentiated current-value event within 7 days;
- ≥40% voluntarily return for current utility.

`MIXED`:
- 25–49% current-value event; or
- 20–39% voluntary return.

`FAIL`:
> value is mostly “useful someday”.

---

## G3 · Recurring value / recurring payment
Run only after G1/G2 survive.

`STRONG` requires:
- real paid continuation;
- continuation driven by current value;
- contribution/retention compatible with G11.

Subscription preference alone is not the endpoint.

---

## G4 · Portability / ownership
Question:
> Does portability materially improve real commitment?

Current cross-front classification:
> commitment/trust gate.

Acquisition caused by it:
> `UNVALIDATED`.

---

## G5 · Non-subscription / hybrid economics
Question:
> Does one-time/lifetime/hybrid reduce rejection while remaining sustainable over 5–10 years?

Must compare:
- cumulative contribution;
- custody/service obligation;
- heavy-user scenario;
- inactive-former-payer scenario.

---

## G6 · Experienced-grower economics
Question:
> Does higher record value compensate for switching/customization/support burden?

Failure does not kill beginner/expanding/professional models.

---

## G7A · Professional workflow

Distribution ≠ payment.

Failure usually kills standalone monetization, not trust value.

## G11 · Economically eligible market ceiling
Authoritative ambition:
> S1 · €75k contribution.

`STRONG`:
> conservative eligible-payer case supports ≥2× S1 requirement.

Examples:
- €22 net → S1 3,410 payers → G11 STRONG 6,820;
- €40 net → S1 1,875 → G11 STRONG 3,750.

`FAIL`:
> even credible upside cannot reach S1.

---

## G12 · Long-horizon funding
Question:
> Can revenue duration fund service-obligation duration?

`STRONG`:
> contribution-positive under conservative 10-year obligation without assuming inactive users pay forever.

---

## G13 · Record-only contribution ceiling
Question:
> Does validated additional value materially improve contribution over record-only?

Do not bundle unvalidated authority to manufacture higher WTP.

---

## G14 · Price sensitivity
Run only after value proposition is fixed and G1/G2 survive.

Use:
- real-money price tests;
- conversion;
- contribution per qualified visitor/user;
- renewal by cohort.

Do not choose final price from stated preference alone.

# 3. Landing-page evidence classification

The landing page can support:

## Acquisition evidence
- visit → weak;
- CTA click → weak;
- email/signup → acquisition signal.

## Commitment evidence
- real tree context;
- starts with one real tree.

## Economic evidence
- voluntary return;
- real payment after current value.

The landing page alone cannot validate:
- G1;
- G2;
- retention;
- WTP;
- G11;
- PMF.

It should feed participants into Step 1 rather than substitute for Step 1.

# 4. Venture kill architecture under S1

## Model kill
A payment/custody/partner/provenance model fails.

## Candidate/segment kill
G1/G2 fail for a consumer segment; G7 fails for professional; G10-B fails for Comparable Journey.

## Venture-level kill

### VK1 · No incremental value
No material dedicated value and no alternative paid job.

### VK2 · No plausible payer
Value exists but no sustainable payment mechanism.

### VK3 · Ceiling below S1
Even credible upside cannot clear €75k contribution.

### VK4 · Unfundable continuity
No trust-compatible model can fund long-horizon promise.

### VK5 · Economics require unvalidated authority / S3 inflation
The model “works” only by assuming future authority or a market expansion unsupported by S1 evidence.

### VK6 · CAC/support consumes the niche
Acquisition/manual activation/support eliminates contribution.

S3 failure alone is not a venture kill.
