# 1. Executive conclusion

W3 does **not** recommend defining Bonsai as:

- a bonsai tracker;
- an organizer;
- a reminder app;
- an AI bonsai expert;
- a care authority;
- a generic social community.

W1 materially strengthens the evidence that **longitudinal recordkeeping is a real job**. Growers maintain records in dedicated apps, spreadsheets, Photos/folders, notes systems, Lightroom metadata, notebooks, paper calendars, phone reminders and hybrid systems. Dedicated-app persistence and paid persistence are observed, but so are uninstall, switching, reversion to general tools and abandonment caused by capture burden. `[Source: W1-FINAL.md §§5–10]`

W1 also establishes a more difficult competitive reality: the current discovered lower bound is **at least 45 distinct competitive bonsai software products/surfaces known to ship or have shipped**, with the exact universe still `UNVALIDATED`. General-purpose and analog tools are first-class competitors, not edge cases. `[Source: CURRENT-EVIDENCE-AND-DECISIONS.md §§2,5; W1-FINAL.md §§3–6]`

The strongest Brand architecture surviving W3 is:

> **KEEP TRACK → SEE PROGRESS → BUILD MEMORY → CREATE CONTINUITY**

Possible later extensions are:

> **LEARNING · STEWARDSHIP · BELONGING**

Those later extensions remain gated by unresolved hypotheses.

### W3 recommendation at closure

| Strategic role | W3 recommendation | Evidence posture |
|---|---|---|
| Immediate/entry value | **Progress** | close to literal customer language; progression value observed |
| Core meaning bridge | **Memory** | strategic synthesis built on observed record/retrieval behavior |
| Leading long-term Brand Territory candidate | **Continuity** | strategic synthesis supported by observed durability/export/lock-in concerns; consumer meaning still `UNVALIDATED` |
| Leading future differentiation hypothesis | **Journey → Learning / Comparable Journey** | progression-as-learning `OBSERVED`; product mechanism `UNVALIDATED` |
| Beginner Brand Principle | **Psychological Safety / Confidence** | beginner public-exposure friction `OBSERVED`; broader effect on adoption `UNVALIDATED` |
| Trust Principle | **Durable Trust / User-Controlled History** | durability/export concern `OBSERVED`; effect on adoption/retention `UNVALIDATED` |
| Long-horizon gated territory | **Stewardship / Living History** | provenance cultural weight `OBSERVED`; consumer H5 `UNVALIDATED` |
| Parked | **Belonging through Trees** | tree-led interaction signals exist; H6 remains `UNVALIDATED` |

This is **not final positioning**. It is the Brand position W3 recommends carrying into Cross-Front Reconciliation.

# 2. What W1 changed for W3

## 2.1 What remains valid from pre-W1 W3 work

### Entry proposition and long-term Brand territory may differ

Still valid and strengthened.

A beginner may enter because they want to keep track, remember what they did or see progress. That does not mean the entire Brand should be built around fear, reminders or task management.

### AI should not be the Brand proposition by default

Strengthened.

W1 concludes that higher-authority care/advice claims carry a larger trust penalty when wrong than neutral record/memory claims. `[Source: W1-HANDOFF-W2-W3-W4.md]`

### Progress is strategically important

Strengthened.

`progress` is literal customer vocabulary, and photo/progression value appears across independent products and general-tool workflows. `[Source: W1-FINAL.md §§6.7,12]`

### Tree Journey remains a useful strategic model

Still valid, but must not be presented as customer language. `Progress` is closer to what users say; `Journey` is W3 framing.

### Brand must not claim practitioner authority it has not earned

Strengthened.

The current Canon continues to treat authority as a constraint, with advice levels 4–5 gated on H7. `[Source: PROJECT-BRIEF.md §§5,8; CURRENT-EVIDENCE-AND-DECISIONS.md §7]`

---

## 2.2 What W1 forced W3 to update

### Tracking becomes stronger as a job and weaker as a Brand territory

W1 makes both statements true at once:

- people demonstrably keep records;
- many tools already help them do it.

Therefore “tracking exists” is not evidence that “a tracker Brand has open space.”

### Organization becomes stronger as a job and weaker as differentiation

Collection organization, retrieval and memory relief appear in W1, especially as collections expand. But spreadsheets, notes, Photos and desktop tools already compete credibly. `[Source: W1-FINAL.md §§6.4–6.6,10]`

### Memory must be split into different meanings

1. **Operational memory**: reminders, dates, what was done.
2. **Historical memory**: photos, progress, reconstructing what happened.
3. **Long-term memory**: history persisting across years.

The first two have direct behavioral support. The third remains a strategic interpretation.

### Continuity is more interesting after W1

W1 observes:

- export concern before committing a collection;
- data-loss concern;
- lock-in concern;
- software/service mortality concern.

W1 explicitly says this supports a **continuity/trust concern**, not validated H5 provenance. `[Source: W1-FINAL.md §6.9]`

### Ownership of history moves from possible consumer territory to Trust Principle

Literal `ownership` is not recurring storefront-review vocabulary. W3 therefore does not treat it as validated customer meaning.

But data portability and control may become evidence of trustworthiness if later product/economic choices support them.

### “Calm organization” is downgraded

`organize` and `one place` are close to customer language. `calm` is not. It remains an optional emotional hypothesis, not evidence.

# 4. Audience Meaning Map

W3 recommends analyzing audiences on two different dimensions.

## 4.1 Bonsai journey stage

1. zero-knowledge / first-tree beginner
2. first / second-tree owner
3. expanding hobbyist
4. experienced / long-horizon hobbyist

Practitioners/teachers remain ecosystem/authority actors rather than the assumed primary consumer target.

## 4.2 Software posture overlay

A separate low-software/analog posture exists. Some growers deliberately use notebooks, paper, spreadsheets, photo systems, metadata or memory. Its prevalence is `UNVALIDATED`; it should not be mistaken for a lifecycle stage. `[Source: W1-FINAL.md §10]`

## 4.3 Meaning map

| Audience | Jobs currently supported | Anxiety/tension | Plausible aspiration | W3 meaning candidates | Do not assume |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Zero-knowledge / first tree** | simple memory, easy first record, reassurance, reminders/basic support | “I don't know what matters”; fear of killing it; intimidation | feel capable enough to continue | **Progress · Confidence · Memory** | provenance, legacy or long-term continuity already matters |
| **First / second tree owner** | keep track, photos, notes, reminders, progress | forgetting actions; not seeing change; fragmentation | see learning and improvement | **Progress → Memory → Journey** | stewardship/legacy is already meaningful |
| **Expanding hobbyist** | organization, retrieval, batching, multiple-tree history, memory relief | collection no longer fits comfortably in memory/current system | understand each tree without administrative chaos | **Memory · Continuity · Progress** | dedicated software automatically beats general tools |
| **Experienced / long-horizon** | detailed records, custom workflows, experiments, photos/history | generic guidance, lock-in, software mortality, data loss | preserve useful history without surrendering control | **Continuity · Autonomy · Durable Trust · Living History** | reminders/beginner guidance remain valuable |
| **Practitioner / teacher actor** | future authority/attestation/teaching role remains unvalidated | reputational risk, incorrect attribution, workflow burden | transmit knowledge and leave trustworthy context | **Stewardship · Trusted contribution** | same consumer proposition should serve them |
| **Low-software overlay** | paper, photos, spreadsheets, notes already solve job | software overhead, subscription, fragility, lock-in | control with minimum maintenance | **Autonomy · Durable Trust** | every grower wants specialised software |

### Audience conclusion

Current evidence does not support one identical proposition for every segment.

W3's strategic interpretation is that meaning may deepen approximately as follows:

> **keep up → see change → remember → preserve continuity**

This sequence is an interpretation, not verbatim customer language.

# 5. Jobs, anxieties and aspirations by segment

## 5.1 Zero-knowledge / first-tree beginner

### Jobs
- understand enough to begin;
- create a low-friction first record;
- remember simple actions;
- see signs of progress;
- avoid feeling publicly incompetent.

### Anxieties
- killing the tree;
- not understanding vocabulary;
- public judgement;
- not knowing what information matters.

### Aspirations
- feel capable;
- see evidence of improvement;
- become someone who can care for the tree with growing confidence.

### Brand implication
Psychological Safety may matter here, but horticultural “safety” cannot become a promise of correctness without authority.

---

## 5.2 First / second-tree owner

### Jobs
- keep track of actions;
- attach photos and notes;
- remember what happened;
- see progress.

### Anxieties
- forgetting dates/actions;
- failing to notice development;
- history spread across tools.

### Aspirations
- recognize improvement;
- build a coherent picture of the tree over time.

### Brand implication
Progress is likely the cleanest entry value. Memory may begin to acquire meaning here.

---

## 5.3 Expanding hobbyist

### Jobs
- organize multiple trees;
- retrieve past actions;
- reduce memory burden;
- batch or streamline recurring records;
- keep photo/history coherent.

### Anxieties
- collection complexity;
- repeated data-entry cost;
- migration burden;
- existing system becoming cumbersome;
- dedicated app failing to justify switching.

### Aspirations
- have enough structure without turning bonsai into administration;
- preserve context across a growing collection.

### Brand implication
This is where Memory and Continuity may become materially more relevant, but economic and behavioral validation remain necessary.

---

## 5.4 Experienced / long-horizon hobbyist

### Jobs
- preserve detailed records;
- maintain experiments/custom workflows;
- retrieve long history;
- keep control of data.

### Anxieties
- data loss;
- software mortality;
- lock-in;
- generic advice;
- forced structure that is worse than existing personal systems.

### Aspirations
- durable, controlled, useful history;
- autonomy;
- potentially richer contextual learning without surrendering judgement.

### Brand implication
Continuity and Durable Trust are plausible here. Provenance/Stewardship remain gated.

# 6. Candidate Brand Territories

## Territory A · Progress

**Role:** entry / early reward  
**Evidence basis:** photo/progression value `OBSERVED`; `progress` is customer language.  
**Brand territory status:** W3 recommendation, not validated positioning.

### Why it survives
Progress can create an early reward before years of historical value accumulate.

### Why it cannot lead alone
Progress is easy for Photos and existing trackers to reproduce. It is relevant but not sufficiently distinctive.

### W3 verdict
**KEEP as entry anchor. REJECT as master territory alone.**

---

## Territory B · Memory

**Role:** primary bridge territory  
**Evidence basis:** record/retrieval/reminder/photo/history behavior `OBSERVED`; “memory” is strategic synthesis.  
**Brand territory status:** `UNVALIDATED` as consumer meaning.

### Why it survives
It connects immediate utility with accumulated meaning:

> remember a task → remember what happened → remember how the tree changed

### Competitive weakness
Generic tools already provide memory. The differentiating possibility is **memory organized around the evolving life of a specific tree**.

### W3 verdict
**KEEP strongly. Best current bridge candidate.**

---

## Territory C · Continuity

**Role:** leading long-term territory candidate  
**Evidence basis:** export, lock-in, data loss and product mortality concerns `OBSERVED`; “continuity” is strategic synthesis.  
**Brand territory status:** `UNVALIDATED` as consumer meaning.

### Why it survives
The history becomes more valuable as it accumulates, while dependence on any fragile service becomes more dangerous.

The territory is therefore not merely “store records,” but:

> **the tree's history remains coherent through time.**

### Credibility constraint
Continuity cannot be promised honestly if the business/product traps records or cannot support durability.

### W3 verdict
**PRIMARY LONG-TERM CANDIDATE FOR RECONCILIATION.**

---

## Territory D · Learning through Progress / Journey

**Role:** strongest differentiation extension  
**Evidence basis:** progression threads as learning infrastructure `OBSERVED`; Comparable Journey product value `UNVALIDATED`.  
**Brand territory status:** gated.

### Why it survives
It could move the venture beyond private storage without pretending to be the expert:

> “Here are relevant documented journeys”

is materially different from:

> “Do X.”

### Risk
Comparability requires context. Enough structure to make comparison useful may increase logging burden.

### W3 verdict
**KEEP as primary future differentiation hypothesis, gated on H6B/Comparable Journey validation.**

---

## Territory E · Confidence / Psychological Safety

**Role:** beginner Brand Principle  
**Evidence basis:** beginner public-exposure friction `OBSERVED`; broader effect on adoption/retention `UNVALIDATED`.

### Credible meaning
> It is legitimate to be learning.

### Not credible yet
> We will keep your tree safe.

### W3 verdict
**KEEP as Brand behavior/principle. Do not use as sole long-term territory.**

---

## Territory F · Durable Trust / User-Controlled History

**Role:** Trust Principle  
**Evidence basis:** export/durability/lock-in concern `OBSERVED`; adoption impact `UNVALIDATED`.

### Strategic idea
Trust may be earned partly by reducing captivity rather than increasing it.

Potential future implications could include export, portability and transparent control, but W3 does not select those product requirements.

### W3 verdict
**KEEP strongly as a Trust Principle required if Continuity survives.**

---

## Territory G · Stewardship / Living History

**Role:** gated long-horizon territory  
**Evidence basis:** provenance cultural/market weight `OBSERVED`; consumer H5 value `UNVALIDATED`.

### Strategic idea
> You may be part of a tree's history without being its entire history.

### Risk
It may be deeply meaningful for exceptional/long-lived trees and irrelevant to an ordinary first-tree owner.

### W3 verdict
**KEEP gated on H5. Do not lead current Brand.**

---

## Territory H · Belonging through Trees

**Role:** parked future extension  
**Evidence basis:** tree-specific update requests and small-group learning `OBSERVED`; H6 product/retention value `UNVALIDATED`.

### Strategic idea
Relationships may form around relevant tree journeys rather than popularity.

### W3 verdict
**PARK until H6. Do not create a generic community proposition.**

- OneNote/Evernote/generic notes;
- Lightroom metadata;

- paper calendars;

## 8.3 Implication for Brand

The Brand cannot rely on category specificity as its advantage.

> **What meaning makes a bonsai-specific longitudinal relationship worth maintaining when good-enough tools already exist?**

Progress helps with entry. Memory provides a bridge. Continuity may provide deeper meaning. Learning through relevant Journey may provide differentiated utility. None is validated as a final position yet.

# 9. Credibility constraints

W3 recommends the following boundaries.

## Credible as current message hypotheses

- keep track;
- see progress;
- keep photos and notes together;
- remember what happened;
- organize multiple trees;
- keep a record.

## Worth testing, not yet proven

- preserve your tree's history;
- keep its journey together;
- build a long-term memory of the tree;
- learn from documented progress;
- keep your history in your control;
- learn without needing to perform expertise.

## Premature / prohibited as current Brand promises

- “Never kill another bonsai.”
- “Know exactly what your tree needs.”
- “We'll tell you the right thing to do.”
- “Expert care for every bonsai.”
- “AI you can trust with your tree.”
- “Verified tree history” as a blanket claim.
- “Learn from thousands of comparable trees.”
- “The permanent home for your bonsai history.”
- “Your bonsai forever.”
- “The bonsai community.”
- “Everything you need for bonsai.”

The Domain Model explicitly states that confidence attaches to individual claims, not to a globally “verified tree.” `[Source: DOMAIN-MODEL.md §7.5]`

# 12. Language and Vocabulary Principles

## 12.1 Prefer concrete verbs early

Preferred:

- see
- remember
- keep
- follow
- record
- add
- look back
- compare
- learn

Use abstract nouns carefully:

- continuity
- provenance
- stewardship
- autonomy

These may govern strategy without appearing in first-contact copy.

## 12.2 Use “tree” externally by default

The internal Domain Model may need `Living Subject` and compositions. Customer language should remain natural unless precision is required.

## 12.3 Use `progress` before `journey` when evidence proximity matters

`progress` is customer language. `Journey` is strategic framing.

## 12.4 Avoid administrative/database language in consumer communication

Use cautiously or internally:

- database
- inventory
- data entry
- logging discipline
- asset management
- record-management system

The Brand must reduce the sense that longitudinal care is clerical work.

## 12.5 Avoid unearned authority vocabulary

Do not currently use as claims:

- expert
- verified bonsai
- correct care
- safe recommendation
- trusted AI
- intelligent diagnosis
- best technique
- guaranteed

## 12.6 Preserve uncertainty

Potential future knowledge language should support:

- probable
- recorded
- observed
- attributed
- confirmed
- unknown

Brand should not cosmetically erase uncertainty to sound authoritative.

## 12.7 Do not infantilize beginners

Psychological Safety means beginner status is legitimate. It does not mean childish, patronizing or unserious language.

# 13. What the Brand must never become

1. **An expert it has not earned the right to be.**
2. **Administrative software disguised with bonsai imagery.**
3. **A status economy where price, awards, prestige or followers define value.**
4. **A beginner-shaming culture.**
5. **A generic popularity feed.**
6. **A data prison whose value rises while user control falls.**
7. **An AI oracle whose confidence exceeds its evidence.**
8. **A complexity-maximizing product merely because the domain model is rich.**

Existing project principles remain compatible with this:

- contribution does not imply invitation for critique;
- story, not price, may be a healthier social currency.

Neither is upgraded to a validated master territory by W3.

# 19. Failure conditions for W3's leading hypotheses

## Kill / downgrade Memory → Continuity if

- longitudinal retrieval has little value beyond short-term reminders/organization;
- users consistently prefer general tools even when Bonsai-specific organization is available;
- Continuity does not materially affect commitment/retention;
- economically viable product/business choices cannot support the durability needed to make the promise honest.

## Kill / downgrade Progress → Journey → Learning if

- progression adds little beyond existing photo systems;
- users mostly seek authoritative answers rather than documented precedent;
- Comparable Journey provides little value without expert interpretation;
- context required for useful comparison makes capture unsustainably burdensome.

## Downgrade Psychological Safety if

beginner intimidation does not materially affect adoption, contribution or retention.

## Kill Stewardship as consumer territory if

H5 shows little provenance/attestation value outside exceptional trees, professionals or institutions.

## Keep Belonging parked unless

H6 demonstrates repeat voluntary tree-led interaction, not generic feed engagement.
