# Bonsai · W4 · Growth Strategy · Current State v0.1

**Date:** 3 September 2026  
**Status:** working reconciliation artifact · W4 not closed  
**Phase:** PRE-VALIDATION / PRE-MVP

## 1. W4 role

W4 is responsible for determining how the venture could plausibly **find, activate and propagate through relevant users**, and which mechanisms also generate useful evidence.

W4 is not currently responsible for:

- selecting P1, P2 or P3;
- choosing Product/UIUX;
- producing a generic channel plan;
- assuming social sharing, network effects or community participation;
- inventing unit economics that belong to W2;
- inventing brand promises that belong to W3.

The organizing question is:

> **Which acquisition moment produces differentiated value before migration and repeated logging costs dominate the experience, and can that mechanism also produce evidence or a propagating object?**

## 2. W1 baseline that materially changes Growth

W1 is formally closed for broad review mining and provides the current behavioral baseline.

### `OBSERVED`

- Growers maintain longitudinal records in both dedicated bonsai products and general-purpose/analog tools.
- Some dedicated-app users persist for months/years; paid persistence exists.
- Some users uninstall, abandon or switch.
- General tools such as spreadsheets, photos, notes and analog systems are real competitors.
- Logging/data-entry burden can cause exit.
- Photo/progression is repeatedly valued.
- Reminders can create value and friction.
- Data durability, export and service mortality affect trust.
- At least four known Android products are in the Google Play 10K+ bracket.
- W1's competitor lower bound is at least 45 distinct bonsai software products/surfaces known to ship or have shipped.

### `SUPPORTED SIGNAL`

- The central tension is cumulative history value versus repeated maintenance cost.
- Beginner, expanding and experienced jobs differ.
- Data portability may reduce adoption resistance.
- Low-friction logging is table stakes, not differentiation by itself.
- Higher-authority care/advice carries a larger trust penalty when wrong.

### `UNVALIDATED`

- H1B prevalence.
- Retention distribution.
- WTP and market size.
- Provenance value.
- Comparable Journey product value.
- Community retention.
- Any final product selection.
- Any W4 candidate Growth loop.

## 6. Candidate Growth mechanisms

### L1 · First-Tree Inception

**Role:** low-migration acquisition + activation.  
**Logic:** new tree → minimal record → immediate baseline/progression value → spontaneous return → journey becomes more useful.  
**Why it survives:** migration burden is near zero.  
**Main risk:** beginner may actually need safety/guidance rather than record/memory.  
**Status:** `UNVALIDATED`.

### L2 · One-Tree Land-and-Expand

**Role:** activation/expansion mechanism, not inherently user-to-user acquisition.  
**Logic:** current-system pain → one tree enters → differentiated value → user voluntarily adds another tree → gradual collection expansion.  
**Why it matters:** directly tests W1's strongest cost/value timing constraint.  
**Main risk:** value only appears after bulk migration or concierge assistance.  
**Status:** `UNVALIDATED`; highest-priority participant constraint test.

### L3 · Progression Artifact

**Role:** object-led propagation candidate.  
**Logic:** fragmented history → coherent progression artifact → owner gets value → artifact travels → recipient meaningfully activates.  
**Why it survives:** progression/photo history is strongly supported as value.  
**Main risk:** artifact produces views only, not follow/create/return behavior.  
**Status:** progression value supported; propagation `UNVALIDATED`.

### L4 · Comparable Journey Contribution

**Role:** discovery + evidence-producing candidate.  
**Logic:** problem/new species/decision → comparable documented journey → useful learning → user later contributes own context/outcome → corpus improves.  
**Why it survives:** progression threads are learning infrastructure and this mechanism can remain descriptive rather than authority-heavy.  
**Main risk:** corpus fragmentation across species × climate × stage × intervention × state; users may consume without contributing.  
**Status:** `UNVALIDATED`; Track A density test possible.

### L5 · Teacher / Club Continuity

**Role:** actor-led distribution + continuity + possible attestation substrate.  
**Logic:** session → work recorded → same tree returns → context improves next session → actor repeats with future participants/cohorts.  
**Why it survives:** recurring small-group learning exists and actor-led distribution can work with low global density.  
**Main risk:** actor becomes a data-entry clerk; economics and willingness are unknown.  
**Status:** `UNVALIDATED`.

### L6 · Transfer / Provenance

**Role:** long-cycle tree-to-person propagation.  
**Logic:** steward A → tree + record → steward B → continuation → future transfer.  
**Why it survives:** provenance/ownership transfer have real precedent.  
**Main risk:** too slow for early Growth and useless if historical logging never persisted; plain transfer already exists in competitors.  
**Status:** `UNVALIDATED`; retained as strategic secondary.

## 7. Cross-loop prerequisite gates

Before a mechanism can be treated as sustainable Growth, W4 requires the relevant gates:

- **G0 · Immediate differentiated value** — value before bulk migration/history accumulation.
- **G1 · Repeat behavior** — spontaneous return, not researcher compliance.
- **G2 · Expansion** — one-tree value causes voluntary expansion where relevant.
- **G3 · Object travel** — artifact/journey reaches another person naturally.
- **G4 · Recipient activation** — recipient does something meaningful, not merely view.
- **G5 · Actor repetition** — teacher/club/nursery repeats without founder doing the work.
- **G6 · Economic viability** — W2 can support the cost structure.
- **G7 · Message/trust viability** — W3 can credibly frame the value.

## 8. Migration and logging kill rule

W4's strongest current constraint is:

> **No candidate should depend on “do substantial work now; differentiated value will appear after months.”**

Migration/logging friction can kill every candidate:

- L1: first record feels bureaucratic; no return.
- L2: one-tree value insufficient; expansion requires bulk migration.
- L3: reconstruction is harder than browsing Photos; artifact is not maintained.
- L4: context-rich contribution is too burdensome.
- L5: teacher/student becomes a data-entry clerk.
- L6: no durable record exists by transfer time.

## 9. Cold-start read

This is structural inference, not a validated numerical threshold.

- **L1:** can work at user 1 if one-tree memory/progression is valuable.
- **L2:** can be fully tested with one user; essentially no network dependency.
- **L3:** owner value can exist before propagation; some viewer/creator density later.
- **L4:** highest density risk because comparability fragments the corpus.
- **L5:** can work inside one teacher/cohort; local density is enough.
- **L6:** low network-density requirement but high time/history requirement.

W4 therefore favors early mechanisms that can create value near zero network density.

It cannot establish product value or prevalence.

3. **L3 · Progression Artifact**
4. **L4-B · Comparable Journey Utility + Contribution**
5. **L5 · Teacher/Club Continuity**

L6 is deliberately later.

## 12. Cheap falsification rule

W4 uses an F0 stage before confirmatory micro-pilots.

F0 is designed to answer:

> **Can the target behavior be demonstrated repeatedly under conditions favorable enough that failure is informative?**

Default screening guide, to be frozen before the individual test:

- participant mechanisms: approximately 8 eligible participants;
- actor mechanisms: approximately 4 independent actors;
- 0 target behaviors → `KILL / PARK`, assuming instrument integrity;
- 1 target behavior → `MIXED`;
- ≥2 independent target behaviors → `SURVIVES CHEAP FALSIFICATION`.

These are screening thresholds, not market estimates and not validation.

A later F1 must tie thresholds to W2 economics and report proper uncertainty where estimating proportions.

## 13. Cross-test controls

- Recruitment source is not Growth evidence.
- Guided activation is not spontaneous activation.
- L1–L3 must not smuggle authority-heavy advice into record tests.
- No forced social sharing, rankings, follower counts or rewards in L3.
- Validation instruments should remain disposable and minimally branded.
- Collect only minimum data required for the hypothesis.
- Invalid test execution is `INVALID`, not `FAIL`.

## 14. W2 dependencies

Before W4 can call any surviving mechanism economically sustainable, W2 must bound:

1. consumer WTP / viable value-capture range;
2. market ceiling and what scale counts as success;
3. acceptable acquisition and servicing cost;
4. long-term storage/export/continuity cost and obligation;
5. teacher/club economics for L5;
6. nursery/retailer incentive if purchase-origin distribution is tested;
7. impact of free/general-tool substitutes;
8. whether partner-led acquisition can support its sales/operational cost.

## 15. W3 dependencies

Before W4 can interpret message-dependent acquisition tests, W3 must resolve or bound:

1. whether the credible territory is progress, memory, organization, story, continuity or another synthesis;
2. how recordkeeping can feel valuable rather than bureaucratic;
3. what durability/continuity promise can be made honestly;
4. beginner reassurance without claiming practitioner authority;
5. what could make a progression artifact worth sharing without status performance;
6. provenance language without prestige/pedigree hierarchy;
7. practitioner/teacher authority without making Bonsai itself the expert;
8. credible differentiation from Photos/Excel/notes without generic “easy logging” claims.

## 16. Market / geography

W4 has **not** selected Brazil, Germany or another geography.

Rules:

- do not pool different markets/contexts into a fictional average;
- workshop evidence is workshop-specific;
- one teacher/club case does not establish actor economics elsewhere;
- Portuguese language and southern-hemisphere climate are not empty territories;
- geography should become Growth strategy only if evidence demonstrates a meaningful distribution difference.
