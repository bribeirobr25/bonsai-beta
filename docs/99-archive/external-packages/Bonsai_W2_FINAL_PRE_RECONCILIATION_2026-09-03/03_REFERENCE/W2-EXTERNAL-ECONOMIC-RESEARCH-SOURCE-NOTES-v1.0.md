## 4. Evidence discipline

External economic research in W2 is always subordinate to:

1. `CURRENT-EVIDENCE-AND-DECISIONS.md`
2. `PROJECT-BRIEF.md`
3. current W1 evidence package

External evidence may establish:
- market precedent;
- current provider behavior;
- observed pricing/model structure;
- current public cost benchmark.

It does **not** validate Bonsai:
- WTP;
- retention;
- CAC;
- eligible market size;
- product choice.
