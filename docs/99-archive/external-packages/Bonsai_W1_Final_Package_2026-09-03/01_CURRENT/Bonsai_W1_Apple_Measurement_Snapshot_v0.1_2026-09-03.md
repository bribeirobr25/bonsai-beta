## Direct implications

1. **Bonsai Album materially changes the old baseline.** Its full listing alone
   shows 192 US, 52 UK and 73 DE ratings.
2. The old statement that BonsaiDo is the US iOS ratings leader is superseded.
3. Among only Bonsai Album + BonsaiDo + Bonsai Care App, the currently observed
   subtotal is already:
   - US: 390
   - UK: 96
   - DE: at least 89 (BonsaiDo DE not re-retrieved)
   - BR: at least 13 (Bonsai Album full BR not retrieved)
4. New 2026 entrants generally have too few ratings for reliable cross-product
   sentiment comparisons.
5. A full fresh census still requires a structured collector or manual
   storefront-by-storefront retrieval for the expanded Apple universe.

## Method limitation

The attempted structured Apple review endpoint could not be directly accessed
from this research environment because URL access is restricted by the
environment. This is **not evidence that the endpoint itself is unavailable**.
The production collector remains technically unverified here.

Therefore:
- public App Store pages remain a valid qualitative source;
- this snapshot is a partial measurement;
- no missing count is silently treated as zero.
