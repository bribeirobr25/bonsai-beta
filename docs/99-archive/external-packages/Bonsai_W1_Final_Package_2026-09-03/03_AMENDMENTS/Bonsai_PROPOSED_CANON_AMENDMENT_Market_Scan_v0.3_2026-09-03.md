## C. Android interpretation

### Current canon
The Android amendment centers on Bonsai Empire at 10K+ installs, with ~42–44K
third-party cumulative download estimates.

### New evidence
First-party Google Play also shows:
- Appy Bonsai: 10K+
- My Bonsai Book: 10K+

### Proposed correction
Keep the Bonsai Empire 10K+ observation and its third-party estimate, but add:

> **Android adoption at the 10K+ download-bracket level is not isolated to
> Bonsai Empire. At least three known bonsai tracking/care products are
> currently in the 10K+ Play bracket. Relative active usage, retention, paid
> conversion, revenue and ranking remain UNVALIDATED.**

### Consequence
The stronger Android signal is now a **category signal**, not merely an
incumbent-distribution anecdote.
