# Bonsai · W2 · Proposed Canon Amendment 001

**ID:** `PROPOSED CANON AMENDMENT W2-001`  
**Date:** 3 September 2026  
**Status:** PROPOSED · NOT YET CANONICAL  
**Origin:** W2 · Monetization & Economics

---

## Current canonical characterization

P3 is described in the current project material as a candidate ceiling with:

> **“Best economics”**

## W2 assessment

W2 finds that wording too strong for the current evidence.

### What is supported

- professional bonsai education and practitioner relationships exist;
- professional bonsai spending exists;
- teacher/student collaboration is being attempted by current products;
- a professional payer could, in principle, support higher contribution per payer
  than low-price consumer tracking.

### What remains `UNVALIDATED`

- recurring underserved teacher/practitioner workflow;
- dedicated-software advantage over WhatsApp, Drive, photos, spreadsheets or course
  platforms;
- teacher/practitioner willingness to pay;
- budget ownership;
- reachable payer universe;
- sales cycle;
- onboarding/support burden;
- CAC/payback;
- whether P3 requires a validated P1 substrate before it has sufficient value.

Therefore “Best economics” currently promotes a hypothesis into a conclusion.

---

## Proposed replacement

> **P3 · Teacher / practitioner layer — Candidate ceiling; potentially higher-value
> professional payer and a possible source of attestations. Economics remain
> `UNVALIDATED`.**

---

## Why this change matters

This amendment does **not** downgrade P3.

W2 ranks teacher/practitioner workflow as the **second-highest economic validation
priority** precisely because it could materially change payer identity, contribution and
ceiling.

The amendment only restores evidence discipline:

> high economic leverage hypothesis ≠ demonstrated superior economics.

---

## Consequence for other workstreams

### W3
Do not assume the professional audience is the primary brand or payer audience until
professional workflow economics are validated.

### W4
Do not treat teachers/practitioners as higher-LTV customers by default. They may be
distribution/authority actors without being paying SaaS customers.

### Cross-Front Reconciliation
P3 should remain live, but its economic superiority must be tested through:

- G7A · workflow value;
- G7B · budget ownership;
- G7C · reachable payer ceiling.
