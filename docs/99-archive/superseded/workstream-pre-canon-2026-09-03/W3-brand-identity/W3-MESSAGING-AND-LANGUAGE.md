# Bonsai · W3 · Messaging Hypotheses & Language Principles v1.1

**Date:** 3 September 2026

## 1. Messaging Hypothesis Matrix

These are test territories, not slogans.

| ID | Hypothesis | Audience | Test | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Keep your trees, photos and notes together. | first/second + expanding | consolidation relevance | commodity/general tools |
| M2 | See how each tree changes over time. | first/second + expanding | early reward | easy to copy |
| M3 | Remember what happened with each tree. | broad | memory vs administration | generic tools also remember |
| M4 | Keep each tree's history together over time. | expanding + long-horizon | Continuity meaning | abstraction/durability obligation |
| M5 | Learn from how trees actually progressed. | hobbyists | learning from documented experience | Comparable Journey unvalidated |
| M6 | A way to learn without needing to already be an expert. | beginner | psychological safety | narrower lifecycle relevance |
| M7 | You are part of this tree's history. | long-horizon | stewardship | grandiose/premature early |
| M8 | Your record should stay in your control. | expanding + experienced | durable trust | requires product/business proof |

## 2. Validation ladder

1. Keep track
2. Progress
3. Memory
4. Continuity
5. Learning
6. Stewardship

Do not use success at L1–L3 as proof of L4–L6.

## 3. Language principles

### Prefer concrete verbs
`see` · `remember` · `keep` · `follow` · `record` · `add` · `look back` · `compare` · `learn`

### Abstract nouns are strategy-first
`continuity` · `provenance` · `stewardship` · `autonomy`

They may guide strategy without becoming customer copy.

### Use “tree” externally by default
Internal object models can be more precise.

### `progress` is closer to customer language than `journey`
Do not report Journey as a verbatim discovery.

### Avoid bureaucracy language
`database` · `inventory` · `data entry` · `logging discipline` · `asset management` should generally stay internal unless a context genuinely requires them.

### Avoid unearned authority
Do not currently claim:
`expert` · `verified bonsai` · `correct care` · `safe recommendation` · `trusted AI` · `best technique` · `guaranteed`.

### Preserve uncertainty
Future knowledge language should be able to say:
`probable` · `recorded` · `observed` · `attributed` · `confirmed` · `unknown`.

### Psychological Safety is not infantilization
Beginner-friendly should not become childish or patronizing.

## 4. Premature Brand promises

Do not use now:

- Never kill another bonsai.
- Know exactly what your tree needs.
- We'll tell you the right thing to do.
- Expert care for every bonsai.
- AI you can trust with your tree.
- Verified tree history as a blanket claim.
- Learn from thousands of comparable trees.
- The permanent home for your bonsai history.
- Your bonsai forever.
- The bonsai community.
- Everything you need for bonsai.


## 5. Cross-front messaging guardrails · added v1.1

### Acquisition language is not a monetization conclusion

W3 now explicitly preserves:

> **message that acquires ≠ value that retains ≠ value that monetizes**

A strong response to `progress` messaging cannot be reported as validation that Progress is what retains or monetizes.

### Continuity wording has a truth ceiling

Until future product/economic design can prove the obligation, avoid unconditional permanence language including:

`forever` · `permanent` · `always available` · `never lose your history` · equivalent lifetime guarantees.

### Comparable Journey language

Prefer descriptive learning language:

- similar documented journeys;
- what happened in comparable cases;
- learn from documented progress.

Do not cross into recommendation language such as:

- evidence says you should;
- therefore do X;
- safest action;
- recommended by Bonsai.

### Authority attribution

Practitioner/teacher/club/institution authority must remain attributed to that actor. Do not convert attestation into generic Bonsai expertise.
