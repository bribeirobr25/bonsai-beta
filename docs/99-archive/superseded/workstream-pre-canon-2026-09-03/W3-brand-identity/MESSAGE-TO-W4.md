# Message from W3 to W4 · Growth Strategy

W3 is READY FOR CROSS-FRONT RECONCILIATION.

W3's surviving architecture is:

> **keep track → Progress → Memory → Continuity**

with `Journey → Learning / Comparable Journey` as the strongest future differentiation hypothesis.

This is not final positioning and does not choose a product.

## What W3 needs W4 to falsify/test

1. **Entry-language performance**  
   Which acquisition/activation framing produces behavior rather than verbal approval: keep track, progress, photos/history, collection organization, memory, or beginner reassurance?

2. **Progression Artifact**  
   Can a progression artifact create a real loop with owner value first and propagation second, rather than merely becoming a share feature?

3. **Practical entry → deeper meaning**  
   Can users enter through concrete Progress language and later experience Memory/Continuity value without needing those abstract concepts at acquisition?

4. **Psychological Safety**  
   Does reducing intimidation/judgement materially change adoption, contribution or return behavior?

5. **Continuity timing**  
   At what acquisition or lifecycle moment does Continuity become relevant enough to affect commitment, migration or retention?

6. **Comparable Journey**  
   Can structurally relevant journeys generate repeated user value and/or evidence value without becoming a generic feed or requiring authority-heavy advice?

7. **Tree-led relevance**  
   Can relationships/propagation form around relevant tree journeys without follower/status/popularity mechanics?

## Guardrails from W3

- do not treat `Continuity`, `Stewardship`, `Comparable Journey` or `Belonging` as validated acquisition propositions;
- do not collapse “message earns click” into “Brand territory validated”;
- do not begin with channel lists;
- keep acquisition moment, activation, propagation and loop distinct;
- preserve W1's customer-language vs strategic-synthesis boundary.

Please return conflicts explicitly for Cross-Front Reconciliation.
