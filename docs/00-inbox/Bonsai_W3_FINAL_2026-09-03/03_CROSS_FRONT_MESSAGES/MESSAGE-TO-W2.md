# Message from W3 to W2 · Monetization & Economics

W3 is READY FOR CROSS-FRONT RECONCILIATION.

W3's leading Brand hypothesis is:

> **Progress → Memory → Continuity**

with `Progress` as the strongest entry/value territory, `Memory` as the bridge, and `Continuity` as the leading long-term Brand Territory candidate.

This is **not final positioning** and does not choose P1/P2/P3.

## What W3 needs W2 to pressure-test

Please return an explicit economics view on the following:

1. **Incremental economic value**  
   Can Memory/Continuity create enough value over free/general tools such as Photos, spreadsheets, notes and paper?

2. **Value timing**  
   What value can be monetized early enough when historical payoff compounds over months/years but capture cost begins immediately?

3. **Long-horizon model tension**  
   Does a recurring subscription create a psychological/economic conflict with a record expected to last for years or decades?

4. **Durable Trust**  
   Can export/portability/user control strengthen trust without making the economic model unsustainable?

5. **Segment threshold**  
   Which segment, if any, reaches enough collection complexity/memory pressure that dedicated continuity becomes economically meaningful?

6. **Truth cost of Continuity**  
   If Brand eventually stands for Continuity, what ongoing costs/obligations follow for storage, export, recovery, migration, support and service durability?

7. **Stewardship/Provenance**  
   Is there any plausible economic value for ordinary consumers, or should it remain cultural/long-horizon rather than monetization logic?

## What W3 does not ask W2 to do

- do not choose P1/P2/P3 on Brand's behalf;
- do not treat paid-use anecdotes from W1 as conversion/ARPU/LTV;
- do not assume Continuity is validated customer language;
- do not optimize for captivity if it destroys the trust proposition.

Please preserve material conflicts for Cross-Front Reconciliation rather than harmonizing them away.
