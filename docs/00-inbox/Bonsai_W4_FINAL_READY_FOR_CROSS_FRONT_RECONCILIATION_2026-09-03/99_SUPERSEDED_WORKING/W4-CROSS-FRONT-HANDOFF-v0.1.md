> **The venture must create differentiated value before migration and repeated logging costs become the dominant experience.**

2. expose value before a large history or collection migration is required;
3. generate useful evidence while acquiring/activating;
4. can be falsified cheaply;
5. do not require Bonsai itself to claim practitioner authority.

## 4. W4 → W2 dependency handoff

W2 should incorporate the following questions into Monetization & Economics:

### 4.1 Consumer / switching economics

- What WTP/value-capture range is plausible when Photos, spreadsheets, notes and analog tools are real substitutes?
- What recurring or one-time economic model is compatible with a benefit that may accumulate over months/years?
- How much acquisition/servicing cost can a user support?
- What economic threshold would make a one-tree land-and-expand mechanism viable?

### 4.2 Long-horizon continuity economics

- What is the cost/obligation of long-term storage, backup, export and continuity if history is part of the value proposition?
- Does a subscription increase resistance because the user fears losing access/history if payment stops?
- Can portability/export reduce adoption resistance without destroying monetization?

### 4.3 Actor-led economics

For L5 and any future nursery-led test:

- Can teacher/club acquisition work at the likely consumer value level?
- Who pays: teacher, learner, organization, or nobody at first?
- What level of manual onboarding/sales/servicing is economically tolerable?
- Is a nursery/retailer incentive plausible if purchase-origin distribution is later tested?

### 4.4 Market ceiling

W4 needs W2 to state what scale counts as economic success. A small, dense actor loop can be strategically excellent and still be too small for the venture's objective.

### 4.5 Output W4 needs back from W2

W4 does **not** need a chosen Growth loop from W2. It needs boundaries:

- minimum viable value per acquired user;
- acceptable cost/servicing ranges;
- actor-economics constraints;
- continuity/export economic constraints;
- market-ceiling implications;
- explicit economic kill conditions for candidate mechanisms.

These boundaries will be used to freeze F1 thresholds after F0.

## 5. W4 → W3 dependency handoff

W3 should incorporate the following meaning/trust questions:

### 5.1 Recordkeeping versus bureaucracy

W1's literal customer language includes:

- keep track;
- progress;
- reminders;
- my trees;
- collection;
- photos;
- notes;
- organize;
- record;
- one place.

Strategic terms such as continuity, ownership of history, calm organization and long-term memory are synthesis, not established verbatim customer language.

W4 needs W3 to determine which territory can credibly make longitudinal recordkeeping feel valuable before history has compounded.

### 5.2 Switching from good-enough tools

W4 cannot simply message “easier logging”. W1 indicates low-friction logging is table stakes and general tools are real substitutes.

W3 should help bound what credible distinction can be communicated relative to Photos/Excel/notes without making unsupported superiority claims.

### 5.3 Beginner reassurance

L1 may involve first/second-tree owners, but W4 record tests intentionally exclude authority-heavy advice.

W3 needs to define reassurance that does not imply Bonsai has practitioner authority it has not earned.

### 5.4 Progression Artifact

L3 depends on an object being valuable enough to travel naturally.

W3 should explore what makes a progression/story worth sharing without turning the system into status performance, ranking or generic social posting.

### 5.5 Continuity / durability

Export/data continuity concern is observed, but W4 treats it primarily as a commitment gate.

W3 should bound what durability/continuity promise can be made honestly without promising eternal service or data permanence the venture cannot guarantee.

### 5.6 Provenance

For L6 and possible L5 attestations, W3 should preserve the distinction:

> evidence/attestation describes confidence in a claim; it must not become a quality badge for the tree or a prestige hierarchy.
