## Proposed amendment register

### `W4-A · Growth taxonomy refinement`

**Proposal:** preserve all W1 evidence but reclassify its W4 handoff items by Growth role:

- entry/switching triggers;
- adoption/commitment gates;
- activation constraints;
- activation/value moments.

**Material proposed change:** `data/export concern` should not be treated by W4 as a proven acquisition moment; primarily treat it as a commitment gate unless future evidence shows acquisition causality.
