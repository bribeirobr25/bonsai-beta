## 1. W3 mandate

W3 exists to answer:

> **What territory of meaning and value could this venture credibly own if it survives validation?**

W3 does not exist to make an unvalidated product sound inevitable.

Its responsibility is to distinguish:

- evidence from interpretation;
- customer language from strategic language;
- entry proposition from long-term meaning;
- usefulness from differentiation;
- trust from authority;
- contribution from status;
- Brand promise from operational truth.

## 3. Current Brand architecture

W3's leading strategic architecture is:

> **KEEP TRACK → SEE PROGRESS → BUILD MEMORY → CREATE CONTINUITY**

This is **not** a validated funnel and is **not** final consumer copy.

The layers have different evidence strength and jobs.

### 3.1 Keep track

Closest to observed customer language and the existing record job.

Role: functional entry job.

Not a Brand territory by itself because tracking is crowded and general-purpose tools compete directly.

### 3.2 Progress

`SUPPORTED` as an entry/value signal and close to literal customer language.

Role: strongest early Brand/value anchor.

Why it matters: visible change can deliver reward before years of historical accumulation.

Constraint: Progress is easy to copy and can be provided by Photos or existing trackers. It is not sufficient differentiation by itself.

### 3.3 Memory

Primary Brand bridge.

`Memory` is **strategic synthesis**, not recurring literal customer vocabulary. It organizes observed jobs such as tracking, retrieval, reminders, photos, notes and looking back.

Role: transform recordkeeping from administration into personally meaningful retrieval around one tree.

Distinctive interpretation:

> not generic memory, but **memory organized around the evolving life of a specific tree**.

### 3.4 Continuity

Leading long-term Brand Territory hypothesis.

`Continuity` is not recurring customer vocabulary. It is supported indirectly by observed concerns around export, lock-in, data loss, software mortality and committing a collection to a fragile service.

Role: give long-term meaning to accumulated Memory.

Constraint: Continuity carries a **truth cost**. It may be Brand territory only to the degree future economics and operations can truthfully support backup, recovery, export, migration, maintenance, support and service durability.

No unconditional permanence language is authorized.

### 3.5 Journey → Learning / Comparable Journey

Leading **gated** differentiation hypothesis.

Public progression behavior supports the idea that documented progressions can function as learning infrastructure.

But `L4-A / G10-A` returned:

> **`MIXED · DENSITY RISK HIGH`**

The first shadow test reached only 12 single-author cases and could not test realistic Tier 1 multi-climate density. `state`, `outcome` and `intent` were present in only 58% of cases. User utility remains `UNVALIDATED`.

Therefore W3 may continue to explore:

> **Learning from documented experience**

but may not claim that useful comparable cases already exist at scale.

## 4. Audience model

W3 uses two dimensions.

### 4.1 Bonsai journey stage

1. Zero-knowledge / first-tree beginner
2. First / second-tree owner
3. Expanding hobbyist
4. Experienced / long-horizon hobbyist

### 4.2 Software posture overlay

A low-software / analog posture exists across stages. W1 observed use of paper, notebooks, spreadsheets, photo systems, metadata and memory. Prevalence remains `UNVALIDATED`.

Practitioners, teachers, clubs and institutions are ecosystem/authority actors, not automatically primary consumer targets.

### 4.3 Segment meaning summary

| Segment | Current jobs/signals | Brand meaning candidates | Do not assume |
|---|---|---|---|
| First tree | reassurance, first record, progress, reminders | Progress, Psychological Safety, Memory | provenance, legacy, continuity already matters |
| First/second tree | track, photos, reminders, progress | Progress → Memory → Journey | Journey is user vocabulary |
| Expanding hobbyist | collection, retrieval, memory pressure, batching | Memory, Continuity, Progress | dedicated app already beats workarounds |
| Experienced/long-horizon | detailed history, custom workflows, durability concerns | Continuity, Autonomy, Durable Trust, Living History | all want specialized software |
| Practitioner/teacher | future attestation/teaching/distribution role | Trusted contribution, Stewardship | payer/economics proven |
| Low-software overlay | spreadsheets/photos/paper/notes | Autonomy, Durable Trust | better app converts them |

## 7. Psychological Safety

Psychological Safety means:

> **It is legitimate to be learning.**

It does **not** mean:

> Bonsai can guarantee the horticultural safety of an action.

Brand must never convert beginner reassurance into unearned care authority.

Beginner-friendly must not become childish, patronizing or low-seriousness.

---

## 8. Durable Trust

The core trust paradox is:

> **The more valuable a longitudinal record becomes, the more dangerous captivity and service mortality become.**

W3 therefore adopts:

> **Trust should come partly from reducing captivity, not increasing it.**

Potential future implications include export, portability, recoverability and transparent control. These are not selected product features by W3; they are truth conditions if Continuity becomes an external Brand promise.

`W4-A` is ratified: export/data continuity is primarily a **commitment/trust gate**, not a proven acquisition trigger.

---

## 9. Authority boundary

W3 can credibly work with:

- record;
- chronology;
- progress;
- retrieval;
- photos;
- what the user documented;
- descriptive comparable cases, if the corpus supports them;
- explicit uncertainty;
- attributed practitioner/teacher/institution claims.

W3 cannot currently credibly promise:

- correct care;
- the right decision;
- safe recommendation;
- expert authority;
- AI authority;
- causal outcome inference;
- blanket verified history.

Authority belongs to the practitioner, teacher, club or institution that actually provides/attests it. The platform may attribute authority; it may not absorb it.

---

## 10. Provenance / stewardship boundary

Provenance carries cultural significance, but consumer value for verifiable provenance remains H5 `UNVALIDATED`.

Attestation means confidence in a **claim**, not quality or status of the tree.

W3 rejects:

- `verified tree` as blanket status;
- pedigree coding;
- prestige badges based on provenance;
- rankings based on institution/practitioner association.

Stewardship / Living History stays gated until H5 provides behavioral evidence.

---

## 11. Community / belonging boundary

Generic community is not a Brand territory.

Tree-specific following, recurring learning groups and update requests are interesting behavioral signals, but H6 remains `UNVALIDATED` as a product/community mechanism.

If social behavior is later explored, W3 principles are:

> **relevance > popularity**

> **progress/story > prestige**

> **contribution ≠ invitation for critique**

Belonging through Trees remains parked until repeat voluntary tree-led interaction is demonstrated.

## 12. Cross-front governing rules

### 12.1 Acquisition is not retention is not monetization

Binding rule:

> **message that acquires ≠ value that activates ≠ value that retains/expands ≠ value that monetizes**

`Progress` may acquire.

`Organization / Retrieval / Memory` may create current utility or return.

`Continuity` may create trust/commitment.

`Comparable Journey` may eventually create recurring differentiated value.

No arrow is validated as a funnel.

### 12.2 Primary joint gate

The load-bearing test is:

> **Can a dedicated experience around one real tree create current differentiated value over Photos / Excel / notes, before collection-wide migration or long historical accumulation?**

W3 representation: `Progress → Memory`.

W4 representation: `L2 · One-Tree Land-and-Expand`.

W2 representation: `G1 + G2`.

### 12.3 Binding Brand kill condition

> **If a dedicated one-tree experience does not create concrete advantage over Photos / Excel / notes under favorable conditions, Brand must not rescue the thesis with Continuity, Journey or Story.**

### 12.4 Comparable Journey kill/downgrade condition

If multi-source / multi-climate evidence cannot support useful comparison density, W3 must downgrade `Journey → Learning`. It may not compensate with broader claims about evidence, learning or community.

## 15. Language principles

### Prefer concrete verbs early

`see · keep · follow · remember · look back · record · add · compare · learn`

### Abstract nouns are strategy-first

`continuity · provenance · stewardship · autonomy`

They may guide Brand strategy without being used as first-contact copy.

### `progress` before `journey`

`Progress` has stronger evidence proximity. `Journey` is a W3 framing.

### Use `tree` externally by default

The Domain Model may use `Living Subject` internally. Consumer language should not inherit entity-model complexity unless required.

### Avoid bureaucratic framing

Do not lead with:

- database;
- inventory;
- data entry;
- asset management;
- logging discipline.

### Preserve uncertainty

Future knowledge language must be able to say:

- probable;
- recorded;
- observed;
- attributed;
- confirmed;
- unknown.

1. **An expert it has not earned the right to be.**
2. **Administrative software disguised with tree imagery.**
3. **A status economy where price, awards, pedigree or followers define worth.**
4. **A beginner-shaming culture.**

6. **A data prison whose value rises while user control falls.**
7. **An AI oracle that hides uncertainty behind confidence.**
8. **A comprehensive super-app merely because the domain is complex.**
9. **A poetic Brand layer used to conceal weak current utility.**

## 17. Validation Landing Page authorization

W3 concludes that there is enough Brand/Marketing material to create a **Validation Landing Page** focused on the contemplated web app, under the following constraints.

### Surface context

- web app;
- mobile-first;
- desktop/tablet friendly;
- presentation context only; this does not select final technology architecture or MVP scope.

### Purpose

The page is an **instrument to test proposition/message comprehension and relevance**.

It is not:

- final positioning;
- final visual identity;
- launch campaign;
- proof that the represented product has been selected;
- permission to claim future mechanisms as existing capabilities.

### Message hierarchy

1. **Progress** — strongest entry/value anchor.
2. **Memory** — explain the benefit of keeping what happened around a tree coherent.
3. **Collection / retrieval** — relevant particularly for expanding hobbyists.
4. **Continuity** — deeper meaning lower in the page, expressed concretely and without permanence claims.
5. **Psychological Safety** — especially in beginner framing.

### Comparable Journey

Do not present as an available proven capability unless the validation instrument explicitly labels it as concept/hypothesis. Current status is `MIXED · DENSITY RISK HIGH` structurally and user utility is `UNVALIDATED`.

### Visual-expression principle for the validation page

Visual direction may be defined enough to create a coherent test experience, but **final Visual Identity remains deferred**.

W3 recommends that the validation page feel:

- calm rather than urgent;
- credible rather than authoritative;
- contemporary rather than rustic/nostalgic;
- observational rather than diagnostic;
- inclusive of beginners without becoming childish;
- centered on the tree and visible change over time rather than generic technology imagery.

These are W3 recommendations for the validation artifact, not a frozen visual identity system.

## 19. Reopen conditions

Broad Brand Territory exploration remains closed unless new evidence materially contradicts the current architecture.

Reopen W3 if, for example:

- L2 shows strong dedicated value but the meaning is clearly not Progress/Memory-related;
- participants strongly and repeatedly articulate a different meaning territory not captured here;
- Memory/Continuity is contradicted by real behavior;
- Comparable Journey becomes strongly validated and overtakes Continuity as the dominant long-term value;
- H5 validates Stewardship/Provenance as a primary consumer driver;
- H6 validates a tree-led relationship model strong enough to change the core territory;
- economics make Continuity structurally impossible to honor;
- a new project-wide Canon amendment changes the product thesis.

Otherwise W3 should refine applications, not generate more abstract territories.

---

## 20. Canonical W3 conclusion

> **People may arrive because they need to keep track and see progress. The strongest current Brand hypothesis is that a record becomes more meaningful when it becomes Memory, and Memory can become Continuity when the history of a tree remains coherent and under meaningful user control over time. Learning from documented journeys is the strongest gated differentiation hypothesis, but corpus-density risk is already demonstrated and user utility remains unvalidated. Psychological Safety governs how beginners are treated; Durable Trust governs how long-term history may be promised. Brand is not permitted to rescue weak product value with more poetic language.**
