**Date:** 4 September 2026  
**Status:** Canonical W3 messaging discipline; not final campaign copy.

## 1. Customer-language hierarchy

### Better attested

`reminder · progress · my trees · collection · photo · keep track`

### Marginal

`record · notes · organize`

### Single instance

`one place`

Do not report the list as equally supported.

## 2. Strategic language

The following are W3 interpretations, not recurring verbatim customer language:

`Memory · Continuity · Journey · Durable Trust · Stewardship · Living History · Legacy · Provenance · ownership of history · calm organization`

## 5. Language principles

### Prefer concrete verbs early

`see · keep · follow · remember · look back · record · add · compare · learn`

### Abstract nouns are strategy-first

`continuity · provenance · stewardship · autonomy`

### `progress` before `journey`

Progress is closer to observed language. Journey is a strategic framing.

### Use `tree` externally by default

Internal domain precision must not make consumer language bureaucratic.

### Avoid administrative framing

Avoid leading with:

`database · inventory · data entry · logging discipline · asset management · record management system`

### Preserve uncertainty

Use concepts such as:

`probable · recorded · observed · attributed · confirmed · unknown`

when future knowledge/evidence language requires them.

### Psychological Safety is not infantilization

Beginner-friendly means legitimate uncertainty, not childish tone.

## 6. Authority boundary

Descriptive:

- what you recorded;
- when something happened;
- photos over time;
- similar documented cases, if supported;
- attributed practitioner claims.

Not currently authorized:

- correct recommendation;
- safest action;
- expert decision;
- AI authority;
- causal inference presented as fact.

## 7. Premature / prohibited promises

Do not use now:

- Never kill another bonsai.
- Know exactly what your tree needs.
- We'll tell you the right thing to do.
- Expert care for every bonsai.
- AI you can trust with your tree.
- Verified tree history as a blanket claim.
- Learn from thousands of comparable trees.
- The permanent home for your bonsai history.
- Your bonsai forever.
- The bonsai community.
- Everything you need for bonsai.

Continuity truth ceiling also prohibits unsupported:

`forever · permanent · always available · never lose your history`

## 8. Cross-front interpretation rule

> **message that acquires ≠ value that activates ≠ value that retains/expands ≠ value that monetizes**

A strong response to Progress copy does not prove Progress is the retained or monetized value.

## 9. Comparable Journey wording

Current status: `MIXED · DENSITY RISK HIGH` structurally; utility `UNVALIDATED`.

If represented in concept tests, prefer:

- similar documented journeys;
- documented cases with related context;
- learn from documented progress.

Do not say:

- evidence says you should;
- therefore do X;
- recommended by Bonsai;
- safest action;
- thousands of comparable trees.
