## 2. Leading architecture

### System A
> **Progress → Memory → Continuity**

- Progress: early reward and entry language.
- Memory: bridge from utility to accumulated meaning.
- Continuity: strongest long-term Brand Territory candidate.
- Durable Trust: condition required for Continuity to be credible.

### System B
> **Progress → Journey → Learning**

- Progress: observed behavioral anchor.
- Journey: strategic structure around longitudinal context.
- Learning: potentially differentiated value.
- Comparable Journey: `UNVALIDATED` mechanism that may enable this path.

Systems A and B are not necessarily mutually exclusive. Reconciliation must determine whether one, both or neither survives economics/product/growth constraints.

## 5. Cross-front Brand kill condition · added v1.1

W4 formally accepted the following W3 constraint for `L2 · One-Tree Land-and-Expand`:

> **If a dedicated one-tree experience does not create concrete advantage over Photos/Excel/notes under favorable conditions, Brand must not attempt to rescue the thesis with Continuity, Journey or Story.**

This means narrative depth cannot substitute for differentiated current value. Failure of the dedicated-value gate must trigger downgrade/reopening of `Memory → Continuity` rather than increasingly abstract messaging.

## 6. Economics constraint · added v1.1

W2 confirmed that Continuity has a real truth cost. W3 therefore treats the territory as credible only if future economics/operations can support backup, recovery, export, migration, maintenance, support and service durability.

`Progress` may acquire while organization/retrieval/workflow relief may retain or monetize. These functions must be measured separately.
